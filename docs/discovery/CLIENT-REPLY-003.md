## Ramallah

**Nabil has managed that branch for nine years.**

For at least four of them, Ramallah has sat in the bottom three of every quarterly comparison I have made. Not catastrophic. Just always slightly behind, every quarter, consistently.

I have had the conversation with him more than once. Not harshly — I like him, and the numbers were never bad enough for anything drastic. But I have asked him what he thinks the problem is. I have asked whether the location is wrong. Last year I asked whether he still wanted the branch.

He never once mentioned the Sunday mornings.

I have been going back through it since your letter, and I think I understand why. **That closure is older than he is in the job.** It is simply what Ramallah does, the way Friday is what everyone does. He would no more think to raise it than he would think to tell me the shop closes at night.

And I never asked, because I was comparing totals and totals do not mention their own hours.

**So: four years of a man being asked to explain a gap that was partly arithmetic.** I do not yet know how much of it. You are right that we cannot know until the hours are measured. But I know it was not nothing, and I know who has been carrying it.

I will talk to him. Not before the review — I want the number first, because going to him with an apology and no figure is just another conversation where I am the one who knows less than he does.

### Something I want to give you

I have four years of quarterly review notes. What I decided, for which branch, and the figures I was looking at.

**Take them.** If the hours let you work out what Ramallah's adjusted position would have been in each of those quarters, I want to know whether the ranking ever actually changed — whether it moved a branch in or out of the bottom three in a quarter where I acted on it.

Not for the dashboard. For me.

If the answer is that it never changed an outcome, I will be relieved and we will have spent a day finding out. If it did, I need to know which quarter.
