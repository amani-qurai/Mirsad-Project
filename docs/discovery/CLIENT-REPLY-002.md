## 3. The trading calendar

Here is the whole of it.

**The week runs Saturday to Thursday. Friday is closed, everywhere, always.**

Our week starts on **Saturday**. The files arriving Sunday is simply the managers sending them the morning after the week ends — it is not the week boundary, and I can see how it would have looked like one.

**Three exceptions:**

- **Ramallah** also closes on Sunday mornings until 12:00. It has for years, it is a local thing, and it means Ramallah trades about half a day less than everyone else every week.
- **Hebron** closes earlier on Thursdays, usually 15:00 instead of 20:00.
- **Jenin** was closed for eleven days in October. Not a holiday and not a decision. It happens, and it will happen again, and I do not get notice.

That last one is the one I would ask you to handle properly. A branch closed for eleven days looks like a collapse in any monthly comparison, and it is **precisely the shape of mistake that nearly cost me a branch.**

I will give you Eid and public holidays as a list, and I need to be able to add a closure afterwards, sometimes weeks afterwards, and have the comparisons recalculate.

Timezone: Palestine. And yes, a sale at 23:30 belongs to that branch's own day.
